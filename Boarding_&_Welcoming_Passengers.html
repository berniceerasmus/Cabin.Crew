1Today's Topic

Today we're focusing on the language cabin crew use when passengers board the aircraft: greeting them, checking seats, and giving polite instructions.

2Grammar Focus: Present Simple for Routines & Polite Requests

We use the present simple to describe routines and procedures that always happen: "Crew greet passengers at the door." / "We check boarding passes."

We use polite request forms to ask passengers to do something, especially "Could you please...?" and "Would you mind + -ing?": "Could you please take your seat?" / "Would you mind putting your bag in the overhead locker?"

Türkçe: Her zaman tekrar eden rutinler/prosedürler için present simple kullanırız (örn. "Crew greet passengers" – Mürettebat yolcuları karşılar). Yolculardan kibarca bir şey istemek için "Could you please...?" ve "Would you mind + -ing?" kalıplarını kullanırız.

4Reading: "A Morning at the Gate"

Elif arrives at Gate 14 one hour before her flight. She checks her equipment, greets the ground staff, and waits for boarding to begin. When passengers start to arrive, she stands by the door with a warm smile and says, "Welcome aboard, could I see your boarding pass, please?" She checks each boarding pass carefully and points passengers toward their seat number.

Some passengers ask for help with their carry-on bags. Elif offers to put heavier bags in the overhead locker so that passengers with window or aisle seats can sit down quickly. She always stays polite, even when passengers are in a hurry or a little confused about where to go. "Would you mind moving to the row behind, please? There's more space for your bag there," she says to one passenger.

Once everyone is seated, Elif and the rest of the crew prepare for the safety demonstration. She checks that all overhead lockers are closed and that aisles are clear. Before the aircraft can push back from the gate, every passenger must be in their seat with their seatbelt fastened. Elif's manager always reminds new crew: "A calm, polite boarding process makes the whole flight feel safer and more comfortable for everyone."

6Role-Play: On the Day

Practise with your teacher or your partner. Try to use at least 2 phrases from the vocabulary box in each scenario.

Scenario 1 — Greeting & Checking the Boarding Pass

A passenger arrives at the aircraft door looking a little nervous. Greet them warmly, check their boarding pass, and direct them to their seat.

Try: "Welcome aboard!" · "Could I see your boarding pass, please?" · "Your seat is just this way."

Scenario 2 — Overhead Locker Space

A passenger's carry-on bag doesn't fit in the locker above their seat. Offer a polite solution.

Try: "Would you mind if I put this in a locker further back?" · "Let me help you with that."

Scenario 3 — Seatbelt Reminder Before Push-Back

A passenger is still standing in the aisle after the seatbelt sign is on. Ask them politely to sit down and fasten their seatbelt.

Try: "Could you please take your seat?" · "We'll be departing shortly, so please fasten your seatbelt."

Scenario 4 — Confused About Seat Row

A passenger sits in the wrong row by mistake. Check their boarding pass and help them find the correct seat without embarrassing them.

Try: "I think this might be the wrong row — could I check your boarding pass again?" · "No problem at all, it's just two rows back."
